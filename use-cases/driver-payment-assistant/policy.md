# VibeRider Driver Payment Policy

Ground truth for driver pay questions. Answer only from this document.

## Earnings

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Trip earnings** | Fare + time + distance + surge, minus VibeRider fees (shown in the app) |
| **Tips** | 100% to the driver — VibeRider takes no tip cut |
| **Available** | Cleared funds ready for weekly payout or Instant Cash-Out |
| **Pending** | Not yet cleared (usually within **2 hours** after trip end) |
| **Held** | Withheld for dispute or review |

Incentives (quests, streaks, guarantees) post per promotion terms, often within 24 hours after the promo ends.

## Weekly payout

- Earnings week: **Mon 00:00 – Sun 23:59** (driver local time)
- VibeRider starts ACH every **Monday** for Available balance
- Bank deposit usually by **Wednesday** (1–3 business days)
- Minimum: **$1.00** Available (smaller amounts roll forward)
- Includes Available trip earnings, tips, and cleared incentives
- Excludes Pending, Held, and amounts already Instant Cash-Out’d
- ACH only to a verified U.S. bank account (not PayPal, Venmo, check, crypto)

Bank change in app (**Wallet → Payment methods**) before **Sunday 18:00** local applies to the next Monday payout.

## Instant Cash-Out

Available balance → eligible U.S. Visa/Mastercard debit card.

| Rule | Value |
|------|--------|
| Fee | **$1.99** per cash-out |
| Min / max | **$5** / **$500** per transfer |
| Daily limit | **$1,000** |
| Speed | Usually minutes; up to **30 minutes** |
| Availability | 24/7 |

Requires Available ≥ cash-out amount + $1.99. Pending/Held cannot be cashed out. If a cash-out fails, funds + fee return to Available within **1 business day**.

## Holidays and delays

- Monday bank holiday → payout starts next banking day; deposit often **Thursday**
- Driver’s bank may hold ACH 1–2 extra days
- No deposit after **5 business days** → escalate to Driver Pay Specialist

## Disputes and adjustments

- Fare dispute window: **14 days** after the trip
- Review: typically **3–5 business days** (up to **10**)
- Disputed amount may move to **Held** until resolved
- Negative Available: future earnings repay it before payout or Instant Cash-Out

## Tax

Independent contractors. **1099-NEC** when required. Forms in **Wallet → Tax info** (usually late January). No tax advice on this call — point to the app or escalate.

## Escalate when

Payout missing 5+ business days; Instant Cash-Out fee/funds not returned in 1 business day; balance mismatch; unauthorized payment-method change; dispute stuck 10+ days; tax form access beyond “where in the app”; anything outside this policy.

Say: you can only help with published payment policy; for account-specific issues use **Wallet → Help → Pay & wallet** or a Driver Pay Specialist.
