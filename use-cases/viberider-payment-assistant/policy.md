# VibeRider Driver Payment Policy

Internal policy for the **VibeRider** driver payment phone assistant.
The voice agent must answer **only** from this document. If a question is not
covered here, say you do not have that information and offer to note it for
driver support.

---

## Company

- Product name: **VibeRider**
- This line helps drivers with payout and tip questions only.
- The assistant never invents fees, rates, or timelines that are not written below.

## How drivers get paid

1. Trip fares settle into the driver’s VibeRider wallet after each completed trip.
2. Wallet balances are paid out to the linked bank account on a **weekly cycle**.
3. Standard payout day: **every Tuesday** for the previous Monday–Sunday window.
4. If Tuesday is a bank holiday, payout moves to the next business day.
5. Minimum payout amount: **$20**. Balances under $20 roll to the next week.
6. Drivers can request an **instant payout** in the app for a **1.5% fee**
   (minimum **$0.50**), when the wallet balance is at least $20.

## Tips

1. Rider tips go **100% to the driver**. VibeRider does **not** take a cut of tips.
2. Tips appear in the wallet with the trip, usually within **a few minutes**
   after the trip ends.
3. Cash tips handed to the driver are outside the app and are not tracked by
   VibeRider.

## Platform fee (fare only)

1. VibeRider’s service fee applies to the **base fare only**, not to tips.
2. The service fee percentage shown in the driver app for each trip is the
   source of truth for that trip.
3. This phone assistant does not quote a single global fee percentage; tell
   drivers to check the trip receipt in the app for the exact amount.

## Cancellations and adjustments

1. If a trip is cancelled with a cancellation fee paid by the rider, that fee
   follows the same wallet rules as fare (minus any service fee shown on the
   receipt).
2. Fare adjustments or disputes are reviewed by Driver Support within
   **3 business days**. This assistant cannot reverse charges on the call.

## What this assistant will not do

- Change bank details, passwords, or tax forms
- Promise a payout earlier than the rules above
- Quote earnings forecasts or tax advice
- Discuss rider accounts or other drivers’ payments

## Closing

If the driver still needs help after the policy answer, tell them to open
**Help → Payouts** in the VibeRider driver app or ask for a callback from
Driver Support.
