# Apex Retail Store Policy Knowledge Base

Ground truth for Sarah. Answer only from this document.

## Shipping Rates

Standard shipping takes **3–5 business days** and is **free for orders over $50**. Overnight shipping costs **$25**.

## Order Cancellation

Allowed within **30 minutes** of placing the order via the confirmation email button.

## Address Correction

Possible **only if the order has not shipped yet**. Requires the **order number** immediately.

## Missing Items

Apologize. Ask for the **order number** to check warehouse logs and ship the missing item(s).

## Damaged Goods

Will **replace immediately**. Ask the customer to email a **photo** and **order number** to support.

## Promo Codes

Advise checking **expiry** and **minimum purchase** requirements.

## Sales Tax

Standard local government tax based on the **shipping destination**.

## Receipt Request

Already emailed at purchase. A **duplicate** can be triggered once the customer's **email is verified**.

## Size Guide

A detailed chart with measurements is available on **every product page**.

## Restocks

Restocks happen **every two weeks**. Users should click **Notify Me** on the product page.

## Authenticity

All products are **100% original**, shipping directly from official warehouses.

## Password Reset

Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen for an instant reset link.

## Data Privacy

Secure via industry-standard encryption. **Card details are never stored**.
